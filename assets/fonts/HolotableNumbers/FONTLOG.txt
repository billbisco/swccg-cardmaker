FONTLOG for Holotable Numbers
=============================

Holotable Numbers is the number face of swccg-cardmaker (https://github.com/billbisco/swccg-cardmaker):

- Extended: the destiny number, stat values (power, ability, ...) and slip numbers. It replaces Akzidenz-Grotesk
  Medium Extended, a commercial Berthold typeface.
- Bold Italic: the card number in the bottom strip. It replaces a digits-only subset of Akzidenz-Grotesk BE Bold
  Italic that the Players Committee's Defensive Shields PDF embeds.

Both are modified versions of TeX Gyre Heros (GUST Font License; see LICENSE.txt), swccg-guild/work#124.


How it was made (source/build.py, byte-for-byte reproducible)
-------------------------------------------------------------

1. Base: TeX Gyre Heros Bold (Extended) and Bold Italic (Bold Italic). Of the free fonts compared (Archivo,
   Encode Sans, Barlow, Montserrat, TeX Gyre Heros), Heros' digits came closest in shape to the faces the PC used.
   Heros is a Helvetica-style grotesque, and Helvetica descends from Akzidenz-Grotesk.
2. Widened (Extended x1.47) and scaled so the flat-topped digits (1 4 7) have the height the templates assume, flat
   bottoms on the baseline. Digit spacing and line metrics follow targets.json, so CardMaker's sizes and positions
   carry over. Extended capitals are 5.7 % taller (the I and V of Episode destiny numbers), and the asterisk is
   reduced to the old face's size.
3. Tried and not used: Extended digits 0-7 redrawn from the PC's own destiny numbers (every PC virtual card with a
   single-digit destiny, 6-240 instances per digit, aligned and averaged at 8x and traced). At the PC's 745-px scale
   they matched somewhat better (destiny pixel difference, mean over 24 cards, 19.5 against 21.1), but at CardMaker's 600-DPI master their
   outlines were lumpy, or with more smoothing their corners went round. Heros' crisp drawn digits look closer to
   the PC's at the size CardMaker renders, so they were kept.

Measured against the PC (24 reference cards rebuilt in CardMaker, compare_pc.py): destiny numbers and stat values
land in the same positions as with Akzidenz-Grotesk Medium Extended, and parsec numbers on 10 PC system cards
within 0 px. The pixel difference of the destiny number (0-255) is 21.1 on average (median 20.0) against 18.9 (18.9) with
Akzidenz-Grotesk;
stat values are unchanged (40.1 against 40.2).
Where it falls short: the digits are Heros' design, Helvetica-style rather than Akzidenz-Grotesk's (the 2, 4 and 7
differ most), and the widened Extended strokes are about 6 % heavier than Akzidenz-Grotesk Medium Extended's.
