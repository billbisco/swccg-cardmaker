# Expansion sphere icons — provenance (2026-11-01)

Two versions per set: `set-<key>-decipher.png` (Decipher as printed) and `set-<key>-pc.png` (Players Committee redraw).
Card key / API `expansion_style`: `"pc"` (default in the card maker since 2026-11-01, Bill: the target look is the PC's) or `"decipher"`.
Remaster builds (build_vs.py, Original Virtual slips) still default to `decipher`; Virtual Block cards (sets 1000+) default to `decipher`.
Reflections Gold has one gold sphere and ignores the style.

Checked 2026-11-01: the PC sheet embeds each sphere at ~57 px, so the x4 files are the best PC source found; the 600 DPI print scan of
Obi-Wan's Journal (Bardez, LS - Device.xcf) gives a ~62 px halftoned Reflections II sphere, no better than the current Decipher file (not used).

## Reflections Gold: remastered gold coin and original Decipher sphere (cut 2026-10-31, added 2026-11-03)

Cut by Alpha2 on 2026-10-31 for swccg-guild/work#50 (the files were only in a scratch folder until 2026-11-03). Both are circular crops with a
4x supersampled anti-aliased edge, then a Lanczos resample to 312 px like the other `set-*-decipher.png` discs. No AI, no retouching.
The code that picks them is not in this commit; `set-reflections-gold.png` above is unchanged.

## Rev 80 (2026-11-12): re-cut from the printed cards (supersedes the table above where listed)

Audit against printed faces (sheets in the rev 80 evidence): the wiki Icon-set-* inner discs were not the printed
spheres, and the PC Game Aid redraws (`-pc`) are not what the PC prints on its Virtual cards (they print Decipher's
sphere art). Re-cut by `tools/set_spheres/cut_set_spheres.py` (sources: `tools/set_spheres/sources.json`): per-pixel
median of the sphere on many printed faces, each centred on its black ring to sub-pixel, scaled by its ring radius and
registered by cross-correlation; cut at 0.92 of the ring's centre-line radius, 312 px, round anti-aliased mask.
